# Prospect Hollow demo

Crystal Cascade opens on an English/French landing page explaining the jewel puzzles and the village. Enter the village, choose a construction, then click the mine entrance or its label to play the next unfinished level. Results return to the village. There is no separate level-selection page or Mine navigation tab: completed levels live in the buildable museum.

**A small village, not a management simulator.** Basic needs and household arrivals are automatic. Any first building has free materials; subsequent projects cost coins. Fund several buildings at once, with one project per plot. Each completed normal puzzle advances every active construction by one step. Services and new inventory capacity activate only when construction finishes. Existing buildings remain useful while being improved. A completed well, farm, and home welcome two residents; the home wing welcomes two more.

| Well                   |        50 |                 2 | Drinking water                              |

| Armory depot           |       350 |                 6 | Capacity of 12 per puzzle bonus             |

The first chosen building’s materials are free regardless of this table. New requirements live in `src/data/town.js`. Each funded project stores its own `required` count so later balancing does not change an existing promise. Earlier unfinished projects retain their original three/four-puzzle duration and saved work. The full catalog has 44 construction steps, but concurrently funded projects share puzzle wins.

**The museum.** Build and select the museum, then open its collection inside the village. Normal replay cards show completed levels, stars, best score, and best active time, grouped by chapter. Normal replays retain completion income, chests, and construction progress. The completed museum is required at the game action boundary, including immediate replay from results. Until then the mine always opens the next unfinished puzzle. After the last campaign level, the mine opens the museum (or selects its plot if it still needs building).

Continuous play offers every unlocked level, including the next unfinished one. It keeps the board playable after obstacles and relic objectives are cleared, without a victory interruption. Scores are stored separately from normal campaign records; it grants no stars, chests, level unlocks, builder hammers, or construction steps. Earn one coin per ten collected jewels, capped at **25 coins per level across all continuous visits**. Reopening the mode or reloading cannot refresh that allowance. Once capped, play and personal-best scores continue without more currency. Earnings and personal bests save after scoring resolutions; the board itself is not resumed after reload. Normal puzzle bonuses use the real inventory. Return to the village/museum whenever desired. This lifetime cap is deliberately simple for the demo, not a daily system.

**Chests and supplies.** One server-replaceable weighted roll determines each score/speed chest before its roulette is shown: 70% puzzle bonus, 20% 25 coins, 10% one builder hammer. Within the puzzle-bonus portion, Clear Row and Shuffle retain their 35% weights, and Hammer, Color Wand, and Tile Breaker retain 10% each. Tier labels/eligibility still follow score and speed; there is one prize per chest. Revealing, skipping, or reopening the results never awards again. The roulette displays the actual saved reward and amount, including overflow conversions.

The initial limit is **3 of each puzzle bonus**, raised to 5, 8, then 12 by completed armory tiers. A full slot converts an incoming bonus into 10 coins. Inventory grants and chest settlement share the same bounded path. The armory panel lists each quantity/limit, and the puzzle toolbar shows the limit. An armory finished by a normal victory provides its new capacity to that victory’s chests.

**Builder hammers are distinct from the puzzle Hammer.** Up to five are stored separately, independent of armory tiers. On an active construction, Use a builder hammer consumes exactly one and advances only that plot by one step; its final step activates the building’s benefit immediately. It never advances other plots, awards mining income, or changes campaign records. Stale commands, missing projects, and empty stock cannot spend. Excess hammer rewards also convert into 10 coins each. There are no free starting builder hammers; testing fixtures may seed earned stock.

**Economy tuning.** Normal completion income remains `50 + min(50, floor(collectedJewels / 2))`. Prices and payouts are intentionally generous while testing the loop. Chest coins and overflow are additional income shown in their reward receipts. Later tune a gradual curve of earnings, costs, and required work against beta feedback, preserving funded projects. Continuous mode has its separate small allowance so it cannot indefinitely fund the village. Keep decisions to one clear action, without maintenance bills, resource ratios, workers, or real-time construction timers.

**3D presentation.** `TownDiorama.js` uses original Three.js geometry for timber buildings, staged construction, animated articulated residents, horses, and a mine cart. The museum has a porch and crystal displays; armory upgrades add supply crates and storage. A separate continuous rolling prairie has winding tracks, branched trees, hills, sparse grass, rocks, cacti, and fog. The landscape is built once per scene. Starting construction rebuilds town objects, preserving the camera. Static geometry is merged by material, shadows update only when needed, ambient animation is capped at 24 fps, and pixel ratio is capped at 1.5. Reduced motion stops ambient animation while manual camera controls remain usable.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use the accessible camera buttons. With the canvas focused, arrows rotate, plus/minus zoom, and Home resets the view. The camera remains above terrain. Labels follow the view and hide when they overlap or leave the frame; every plot also remains in the building list. Dragging and pinching do not select plots or enter the mine. Museum dialogs and Settings pause the town. Renderers stay outside Vue’s reactive graph and dispose controls, geometry, materials, textures, observers, and WebGL contexts on exit. The SVG fallback supports the same construction, museum, armory, and mining actions when WebGL is unavailable. The landing illustration reuses original SVG building art and loads no 3D renderer.

**Persistence and accounting.** `localProfile` is the replaceable storage boundary; there is no backend or account requirement. The profile stays under `crystal-cascade-profile-v2` with normal/continuous records, wallet, bounded powers, builder hammers, building stages, projects, events, and run markers. Earlier single-project and campaign-only saves migrate with their recovery key intact. Saved over-cap inventory is converted once into 10 coins per excess item, with a visible explanation; saving/reloading retains the normalized quantities and coin balance. Normal victory settles the campaign, construction, new capacity, chest awards, and mining payout together before presenting results. Continuous receipts validate the active run and track credited earnings independently. Duplicate or stale settlement cannot pay twice.

**Language and tester reset.** All landing, town, museum, armory, puzzle, chest, Settings, sign, and accessibility text follows the first supported browser preference (French or English, otherwise English). A future account setting can use the same locale boundary. Settings → Reset progress for testing asks for confirmation and clears normal/continuous records, allowance usage, powers, hammers, money, buildings, work, and events. Cancellation preserves everything. The confirmed reset returns to the bare village and persists across reload.

**Optional bandit story.** After the first household arrives, a warned, optional encounter resolves once. A completed sheriff prevents theft; an unfinished sheriff does not. Otherwise loss is capped at ten coins or ten percent of the wallet, keeping at least fifty coins when available. No raids occur while away. Reset allows testers to repeat the story. Modernization, cars, further characters, and more chapters remain future content.

**Verification.** The test suite covers varied/concurrent construction, legacy durations, last-step benefits, hammer spending and stale requests, every bonus capacity, overflow conversion, reward weighting, atomic coin/hammer settlement, museum eligibility, continuous play past objectives, lifetime allowance across retries/reloads, separate scores, and reset. Existing engine, fusion, input, active-time, reward, camera-clearance, and localization regressions remain in place. The parallel main branch’s expanded fusion behavior and French feedback are preserved.

All 246 tests across 18 files pass, along with formatting and the production build. Real browser play completed normal puzzles and continued for five moves after clearing continuous-mode objectives. The final six-color production run cleared level one in nine mouse moves, saved the well at 1/2 steps, and offered level two at the mine. French desktop/phone checks covered museum modes, armory/hammer interactions, coin/hammer roulette fixtures, capped continuous receipts across reload and repeat visits, and reset confirmation/cancellation. Browser screenshots and fixture details are recorded in the PR. Physical-device performance and beta feedback still need evaluation. Progress belongs to this browser; clearing its data can lose the save. Local state is editable and is not an anti-cheat boundary. The separate Symfony work in issue #10 will provide recoverable accounts and authoritative action/reward accounting.
