Prospect Hollow is a small town-building story alongside Crystal Cascade. The player chooses a bare plot, completes normal jewel puzzles, and watches a building take shape. Players can fund several buildings at once, with one project per plot and a single coin payment for materials. There are no construction timers, production rates, worker assignments, or offline penalties.

**Try it.** Run `npm ci` and `npm run dev`. Choose any plot in the 3D scene or the equivalent building list, then Start building. Materials for the first project are free. Click the mine entrance or its label to play the next unfinished level. Each successful puzzle advances every active project once, including completed replays. Return through Visit town to see the new work. Three wins complete a new building; four complete the additional home improvement. Wins without an active project still earn normal coins and puzzle rewards.

The well, farm, home, saloon, stables, and sheriff can be chosen in any order. Subsequent projects pay their displayed materials cost up front. Several projects can run together when the player can afford their materials. The first free start is used immediately, so further starts are paid even before that first building is complete. The stages are marked plot/materials → foundation → timber frame/scaffolding → completed building. The home's extra wing takes four wins. Building services and residents activate only on completion: the first household needs a completed well, farm, and home; the larger home accommodates another household. Starting or partially completing a project grants none of those benefits. Cosmetic townspeople, including Ada, are visitors rather than counted households.

**Language and testing reset.** The entire app follows the first supported language in `navigator.languages`: French regional variants select French, English variants select English, and other preferences fall back to English. Town dialogue, 3D signs, map labels, puzzle instructions, chapter/level names, chests, settings, and accessibility text share `src/i18n`. The document language and number formatting match. No local language override is saved. A future backend preference can call the same `setLocale` boundary. Settings contains Reset progress for testing, with a confirmation step. It resets campaign, power inventory, coins, buildings, construction, and events on this device and returns to the bare town; cancelling changes nothing.

**3D presentation.** `TownDiorama.js` creates original, editable Three.js geometry: a raised terrain base, timber buildings, exposed construction frames, recessed windows, roofs, garden beds, trees, mine rails/cart, horses, and a small articulated cast. Residents have separate hip, knee, shoulder, elbow, and head joints, with walking, breathing, looking around, greeting, and farm-work poses. The warm directional light casts building shadows; moving characters have inexpensive contact shadows. Static meshes merge by material and their shadow map is refreshed only when the scene changes. Rendering is capped at 24 frames per second and device pixel ratio at 1.5.

`TownScene.vue` owns the renderer outside Vue reactivity and projects accessible HTML labels over the scene. Clicking either the mine model or its label starts the next level. It pauses animation with settings, page visibility, and reduced motion; leaving town stops the animation loop and disposes geometry, materials, textures, observers, and the WebGL context. Three.js and Phaser are loaded separately for their respective views. If WebGL is unavailable, the selectable SVG map provides the same plot, construction, and mining actions. SVG panel previews show the current work and the eventual building.

**Saving and accounting.** The local profile remains under `crystal-cascade-profile-v2`; it stores campaign records, best times, powers, wallet, completed building stages, a projects map keyed by building ID, each with its target stage and completed-win count, events, and run settlement markers. Existing town saves retain completed buildings. Earlier single-project saves migrate their unfinished work into the projects map without losing completed wins or granting another free start. A legacy `crystal-cascade-campaign-v1` save migrates when no new profile exists; its original key remains a backup. Explicit reset writes a fresh current profile, so the retained migration backup is not loaded again during normal startup.

A victory commits puzzle rewards, coins, construction work, and any completed building together, before result animations. Duplicate/stale completions cannot add another construction step or another payout. Starting a project validates its displayed stage, available coins, and absence of an active project on that same plot. Repeated clicks cannot spend twice. Partial work persists across reloads, and abandoned puzzles award no work. Storage failures are reported; unreadable/future-version saves are preserved until an explicit reset.

This demo is local and intended for one active tab. Clearing browser data or losing the device can lose progress, and local data is editable. No account, API, database, or local tamper protection is provided. Symfony cloud persistence and authoritative actions remain in [issue #10](https://github.com/Starbugstone/Crystal-Cascade/issues/10); its eventual settlement should also include construction work and the account language preference.

**Demo economy and the later progression curve.** Completed puzzles currently pay `50 + min(50, floor(ordinaryJewelsRemoved / 2))` coins, alongside existing chests. Count actual ordinary jewels removed through matches, cascades, and powers; exclude special pieces, obstacles, generation, and reshuffling alone. The generous income is intentional for testing. Materials cost 50 for the well, farm, and home; 100 for saloon, stables, and sheriff; 150 for the extra home wing. The first chosen project's materials are waived. The six buildings and home improvement require 22 construction steps in total. Since one puzzle advances every active project, concurrent construction can finish the town in fewer runs than building everything one at a time; timing depends on materials and start order.

Before release, tune a gradual progression curve from beta feedback: quick early projects, increasing work requirements for later chapters, and materials prices matched to expected earnings. Keep the next improvement understandable and achievable over a few sessions; measure wins-to-completion, wallet surplus, project abandonment, and return visits. Do not scale every number at once, introduce expert resource balancing, or add waiting/offline punishments. Preserve earned progress when tuning existing projects. Modernization, early cars, and additional short stories remain future content in [issue #9](https://github.com/Starbugstone/Crystal-Cascade/issues/9).

**Optional trail story.** Once the first household arrives, Stories from the trail offers a warned, optional bandit encounter. A completed sheriff prevents theft; an unfinished sheriff does not. Otherwise the loss is capped at 10 coins or 10% of the balance, retaining at least 50 coins when available. It resolves once per profile and never runs while the player is away. A reset allows testers to repeat either outcome.
