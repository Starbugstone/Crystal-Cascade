Village audio credits

Recorded music and village life for Prospect Hollow. Thank you to the musicians and field recordists who made these sounds available.

  • Music: “Porch Swing Days – slower” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Full acoustic performance, loudness adjusted, with a gentle entrance fade; encoded as MP3.
  • Birds: “Ambient Bird Sounds” by isaiah658, CC0. Trimmed, filtered, loudness adjusted and crossfaded into a loop.
  • Distant crowd: “Outside Talks #6” by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Outdoor French conversations, excerpted, filtered for distance, loudness adjusted and crossfaded into a loop.
  • Construction: “Fast Hammer SFX” by themightyglider, derived from bart’s “68 Workshop Sounds”, CC0. Filtered, loudness adjusted and faded.
  • Mining: “Mining Pick” by spookymodem, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Filtered, loudness adjusted and faded.
  • Horse: “Little neighing call #6” by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Filtered, loudness adjusted and faded.
  • Hooves: “Horse gallop, tall grass” by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Seven-second excerpt, filtered, loudness adjusted and faded.
  • Raid warning: “Shot of Winchester Magnum XTR” by Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Filtered and softened for a distant warning, with entrance and tail fades.

All effects are encoded as 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps MP3 files. Asset licenses apply independently of the application’s source code license. Source pages and licenses checked on 6 September 2026.
